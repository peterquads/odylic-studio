retail locations across the United States — every Apple Store, and Aesop's US signature stores.

Apple · stores

From the All The Places open dataset, which scrapes Apple's own store pages. Names, street addresses, phone numbers and coordinates are Apple's own, surveyed to the storefront.

This is a 2022 snapshot — the most recent one reachable from this environment. Apple currently reports roughly 270 US stores, so a handful of openings, closures and relocations since 2022 will not be reflected.

Aesop · stores

Compiled from Aesop's public store-locator pages. Aesop's own locator API is not reachable from this environment, so these were gathered store by store.

Aesop's locator currently reports 78 US locations, so this layer is a substantial sample rather than the complete set — mostly missing smaller-market stores. Coordinates are street- or mall-level, accurate to about a block, not surveyed to the storefront.

Projection

Albers equal-area conic, with Alaska and Hawaii inset at their conventional positions. Distances between insets and the mainland are not to scale.
